# 🧠 FieldMind

### Every incident teaches the next one.

FieldMind is an AI operational-memory agent that remembers what happened, learns what worked and failed, and helps field teams make better decisions when similar incidents happen again.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Field teams in areas such as water management, electricity, infrastructure, and emergency operations deal with recurring incidents.

But valuable operational knowledge is often scattered across:

- Incident reports
- Maintenance records
- Field notes
- Messages
- Individual team members' experience

When a similar incident happens again, teams may repeat previous troubleshooting steps without knowing what worked or failed before.

**FieldMind turns these past experiences into persistent organizational memory.**

---

## 💡 The Solution

FieldMind follows a simple learning cycle:

**Incident → Action → Outcome → Learning → Future Decision**

When a new incident is reported, FieldMind:

1. Stores the incident in long-term memory.
2. Recalls relevant historical incidents using Hindsight.
3. Identifies recurring patterns.
4. Compares previous successful and unsuccessful interventions.
5. Generates an evidence-based recommendation.
6. Stores the new incident so it can influence future decisions.

---

## 🧠 Why Hindsight?

Hindsight is the core memory layer of FieldMind.

Instead of treating every incident as an isolated conversation, Hindsight allows FieldMind to build persistent organizational memory.

### FieldMind uses Hindsight for:

- **Retain** — Store new field incidents.
- **Recall** — Retrieve relevant historical experiences.
- **Memory-based reasoning** — Give the AI access to previous incidents and outcomes.
- **Continuous learning** — New incidents become part of the organization's future knowledge.

This creates a memory loop:

```text
New Incident
     ↓
Hindsight Memory
     ↓
Historical Recall
     ↓
Pattern Detection
     ↓
Better Recommendation
     ↓
New Learning
     ↓
Hindsight Memory
